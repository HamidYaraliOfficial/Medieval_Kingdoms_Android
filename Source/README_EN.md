# Iron Crown: Medieval Kingdoms

**Developer:** Hamid Yarali (حمید یارعلی)
**Copyright © 2026 Hamid Yarali. All Rights Reserved.**

A 3D medieval real-time strategy game: gather resources, build a village and a castle, train an army, research
technologies and conquer rival kingdoms. Every model, texture and sound is generated by the game itself — there are no
third-party game assets and no commercial names.

## Quick start (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

| You want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Play right now, nothing installed | Extract the ZIP, double-click **RUN.bat** (starts `Game\Game.exe`) |
| Install with shortcuts and an uninstaller | Double-click **INSTALL.bat** (starts `Installer\GameSetup.exe`) |
| Rebuild from source | Double-click **BUILD.bat** (needs Node.js; see BUILD_GUIDE.md) |

The executables are not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC": click
**More info → Run anyway**.

Requirements: Windows 10/11 x64, a GPU/driver with WebGL 2 (any recent integrated GPU works), 4 GB RAM, about 350 MB of disk
space (the portable folder) — the installer needs about the same.

Save games and settings are stored in `%APPDATA%\IronCrown-MedievalKingdoms\data`.

## Game modes

* **Campaign** — 7 missions: A Humble Beginning, The Frozen Pass, Across the River, Gold of the Forest, The Rocky Fortress,
  Desert Sands, The Iron Crown. Objectives are shown on screen; resources carry over as a reward for finishing a mission.
* **Skirmish** — pick a map, size, number of enemy kingdoms and AI difficulty (Easy, Normal, Hard, Expert).
* **Free Build** — no enemies; build and experiment in peace.
* **Custom Battle** — choose attacker or defender, the fortification (open field, light fort or great castle) and army size.

7 hand-built maps plus a random-map generator, in three sizes.

## What is in the game

* **8 resources:** wood, stone, iron, gold, food, wheat, flour and bread (wheat → flour at the mill → bread at the bakery).
* **31 buildings** — economic, military, defensive and administrative. Place with rotation, upgrade (3 levels),
  demolish (partial refund) and **relocate** finished buildings (a quarter of the cost, half the construction work is kept).
* **13 unit types:** villager, swordsman, heavy swordsman, shield bearer, spearman, royal guard, archer, crossbowman,
  light cavalry, heavy cavalry, catapult, battering ram, siege tower — with walking, fighting and working animations.
* **Army control:** box select, groups (Ctrl+1…9), 6 formations, 3 stances, patrol, attack-move, garrison, retreat.
* **Siege:** walls, gates, towers, moats and bridges; rams, catapults and siege towers; garrisoned archers.
* **30 technologies** (13 economy, 17 military) in three tiers, researched in the town buildings.
* **Kingdom overview (F4):** resource balance, taxes, market trading and rival kingdoms. Population and food supply are
  always visible in the top bar.
* **AI opponents** that build an economy, train armies, attack in waves and lay siege.
* **Weather and time:** clear, rain and storms, plus a day/night cycle.
* **Interface:** resources, population, minimap, game speed (pause/1×/2×/3×), objectives, notifications, build menu and
  command panel.
* **Languages:** English and Persian (right-to-left layout, Persian digits).
* **Audio:** original procedural music, effects and ambience with separate music / effects / ambient volume sliders.
* **Settings:** graphics presets (Low, Medium, High, Ultra), resolution, fullscreen, shadows, anti-aliasing, FPS limit,
  edge scrolling, rebindable keys.
* **Saving:** 8 versioned save slots, autosave, quick-save (F5) and quick-load (F9).

Controls are listed in **CONTROLS.md**.

## Technical notes (please read)

* **Engine:** the game is written in JavaScript with three.js (WebGL) and packaged for Windows with Electron. It was
  *not* made with Godot: Electron + three.js could be built, run and tested headlessly in the build environment and gives
  native right-to-left text for Persian.
* **What has been tested:** the game logic (simulation, AI, saves, campaign flow) and the complete user interface were
  tested by running the real game in headless Chromium with software WebGL, including: menus, new game flows, building
  placement and relocation, training, research, selection and orders, save/load, English and Persian layouts, pause,
  victory and defeat screens, battles and sieges.
* **What has NOT been tested:** `Game.exe` and `GameSetup.exe` were produced on Linux (official Electron Windows runtime,
  NSIS `makensis`) and could not be executed in the build environment, because no Windows machine was available. Their
  structure was checked (valid PE files, icon and version information present, installer payload lists every file), but
  they have never been launched on real Windows. Audio was verified to run without errors but could not be listened to.
  Performance on real GPUs was not measured (tests ran with a software renderer at about 1–2 fps).
* **Balance:** the AI economy is tight, battles are very lethal and difficulty values are first-pass; expect rough edges.
